What CrashPulse does

CrashPulse is a menu bar app for macOS that records sustained CPU activity and suggests which app was most likely responsible. Its figures, including CPU energy, are estimates based on what macOS makes available to ordinary apps, and some processes that belong to macOS cannot be measured. Use its reports as a guide, not as proof of what caused a problem. CrashPulse never stops, slows down or changes other apps.
